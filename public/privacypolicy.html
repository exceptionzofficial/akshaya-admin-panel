Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

Welcome to Satvamirtham. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Customer App and Rider App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Location Data:
    • For Customers: We collect precise or approximate location data to facilitate order delivery and show nearby restaurants.
    • For Riders: We collect precise location data (even when the app is in the background) to track delivery progress, assign nearby orders, and provide navigation.
  • Transaction Data: Details about the orders you place and payment methods used (we do not store full credit card details; these are handled by secure payment gateways).
  • Device Information: IP address, device type, operating system, and app usage statistics.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • To process and deliver your food orders.
  • To enable real-time tracking of deliveries for both customers and administrators.
  • To manage your account and provide customer support.
  • To send transaction notifications (order confirmation, delivery status).
  • To improve our app performance and user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraudulent activities.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following cases:

  • With Delivery Partners (Riders): We share the customer's name, phone number, and delivery address with the assigned rider to fulfill the order.
  • With Service Providers: We use third-party vendors for payment processing, cloud hosting (AWS), and push notifications (Firebase).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, we may disclose information to government authorities.

4. Background Location (Rider App)

The Satvamirtham Rider App collects location data to enable order tracking and efficient assignment of delivery tasks even when the app is closed or not in use. This data is essential for the core functionality of the delivery service.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure server protocols, to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email provided below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
